NoteForge - Melodyne-style vocal editing + live auto-tune for FL Studio
=====================================================================
Windows 10/11, 64-bit, VST3. Nothing else needs to be installed.

INSTALL
  1. Close FL Studio.
  2. Double-click "Install NoteForge.bat" and click Yes when Windows asks for permission.
  3. Open FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins".
  4. Add NoteForge as an effect on your vocal's mixer track (first slot, before reverb/delay).

  (Manual install: copy the "NoteForge.vst3" folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\)

EDIT A VOCAL (Melodyne style)
  - Drag a vocal file (WAV/AIFF/FLAC/OGG/MP3) onto NoteForge, then press play once:
    NoteForge finds where the file sits in your song by itself.
  - Or click TRANSFER and play the part you want to edit.
  - Drag notes up/down to change pitch, drag their edges to change timing, use the tools
    (Pitch, Time, Vibrato, Drift, Formant, Amp, Split) and "Correct Pitch..." for one-click tuning.
  - The small piano in the toolbar chooses which notes it tunes to. Click a key on the big
    keyboard to hear that note.

LIVE AUTO-TUNE (while recording)
  - Click LIVE. Engine "Low latency (recording)" while you record, "High quality (mixing)" for the mix.
  - Voice range "Auto" works for any voice. BYPASS lets you compare with and without tuning.
  - Turn off direct monitoring on your audio interface, use an ASIO buffer of 128-256 samples,
    and turn off "Smart disable" for NoteForge.

OPTIONAL: LET NOTEFORGE MOVE FL'S PLAYHEAD
  1. Install loopMIDI (tobias-erichsen.de/software/loopmidi.html), create a port named NoteForge.
  2. FL Studio > Options > MIDI settings > Input: select "NoteForge", click Enable,
     Controller type: "NoteForge Bridge (user)".
  3. The "FL link" button in NoteForge turns green. Now the marker tab on the ruler moves FL's
     playhead and the Play button starts FL from it.

TIPS
  - Hover over any knob or button for ~2 seconds to see what it does.
  - Right-click a knob or slider to reset it or type a value.
  - Mouse wheel on the time ruler zooms, like FL.

Piano sounds: Steinway Model B recordings from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios
(theremin.music.uiowa.edu), free for any use.
